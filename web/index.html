Search syntax
  • water — matches articles containing "water"
  • climate AND carbon — both terms must appear
  • climate OR carbon — either term
  • water NOT carbon — has "water" but not "carbon"
  • (climate OR forest) AND NOT pollution — group with parentheses
  • "carbon capture" — exact phrase
  • Plain words next to each other are AND-ed by default.

    Ask your archive

    Keys/settings are saved only in this browser's local storage and sent directly from your browser to the provider you choose — never stored in the repo. Don't have Groq/Gemini or a local server set up? Use "⧉ Copy prompt" below instead — it builds the same search-then-prompt from your archive and puts it on your clipboard so you can paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or anywhere else and paste the answer back here.

    Ask a question about what's in the archive — e.g. "what's changed about India's water crisis over the last two years?" It searches the archive first, then asks the AI to answer using only what it finds, with sources.

    Today's brief

    Auto-generated once a day from your unread articles from the last few days — the same "find what's genuinely worth noticing" reasoning as ⚡ Tensions, but picked for you instead of you setting a scope. Uses the AI provider configured in 💬 Ask. Already-shown items won't repeat in a future brief.

    Tension finder

    Pulls recent articles from your archive and asks the AI to find genuine tensions in them — a surprising stat, a gap between a headline number and lived reality, a contradiction between two sources. Built for curiosity-driven storytelling, not a news summary. Uses the same AI provider configured in 💬 Ask (open that panel's ⚙ if you haven't set one up yet).
